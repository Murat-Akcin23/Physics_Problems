### 1. Projectile Motion (Eğik Atış)
*   **Initial Velocity:** $v_x = 80 \, m/s$, $v_y = 60 \, m/s$
*   **Time of Flight:** $T = \frac{2 \times v_y}{g} = \frac{120}{10} = 12 \, s$
*   **Max Height:** $H = \frac{v_y^2}{2g} = \frac{3600}{20} = 180 \, m$
*   **Range (Menzil):** $R = v_x \times T = 80 \times 12 = 960 \, m$
*   **Explanation (EN):** "I decomposed the velocity vector into x and y components to find the flight time and range."

### 2. Newton’s Second Law ($F = m \cdot a$)
*   **Question:** 5 kg mass, 20 N force. Find acceleration ($a$).
*   **Solution:** $a = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{20}{5} = 4 \, m/s^2$
*   **Explanation (EN):** "According to Newton's Second Law, acceleration is force divided by mass."

### 3. Momentum Conservation (Çarpışma)
*   **Question:** 70 kg runner (3 m/s) jumps on 140 kg cart.
*   **Solution:** $m_1 v_1 = (m_1 + m_2) V_{final} \implies 210 = 210 \cdot V_{final} \implies V_{final} = 1 \, m/s$
*   **Explanation (EN):** "Momentum is conserved. As the total mass increased, the final velocity decreased proportionally."

---

### 4. Wave Properties (Dalgalar)
*   **Question:** $v = 340 \, m/s$, $f = 170 \, Hz$. Find wavelength ($\lambda$).
*   **Solution:** $\lambda = \frac{v}{f} = \frac{340}{170} = 2 \, m$
*   **Explanation (EN):** "Wavelength is calculated by dividing the wave speed by its frequency."
